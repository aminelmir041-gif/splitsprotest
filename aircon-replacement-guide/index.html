The first warning

She's been on borrowed time for a while, hasn't she?

Every summer, same story. You turn her on, hear a noise you'd normally report to the mechanic… and hope for the best.

And fair enough. If it still cools, why touch it?

The sensible bit

Before you defect it, check the basics.

Not every tired aircon needs replacing. A dirty filter can make a decent system work harder than it should.

  • Check the filters. Clean them according to the manufacturer's instructions, especially during heavy use.
  • Give the outdoor unit some breathing room. Remove leaves and anything obstructing airflow.
  • Use COOL mode. Setting the remote to 16°C doesn't normally make the room cool any faster.

If it's icing up, leaking, tripping power or struggling after basic maintenance, get a qualified technician to check it.

And no, a can of deodorant sprayed into the vents isn't a deep clean.

“MATE, THAT'S NOT A FILTER. THAT'S A CRIME SCENE.”

The decision

But if it's old… why risk it?

An ageing aircon can keep going for years. It can also pack it in when you need it most. That's the gamble.

Replacing a struggling unit before peak heat gives you time to choose the right size, plan the installation and avoid the last-minute scramble.

A suitable modern system may also give you quieter running, improved efficiency, more consistent comfort and a fresh manufacturer's warranty. Exact running costs and savings depend on the units and how you use them.

Think of it this way: you don't have to wait until the car breaks down on the M4 before admitting it's on its last legs.
