Privacy Policy

This policy explains how SplitsPro handles personal information from customers, website visitors and people applying to work with us, including through Facebook and Instagram lead forms.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

1. Who we are

SplitsPro is an Australian air-conditioning supply, installation and service business. You can contact us about privacy at info@splitspro.com.au.

2. What information we collect

Depending on your interaction with us, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, email address, suburb, service address and enquiry details.
  • Air-conditioning requirements, booking preferences, messages, photos and information relevant to a quote or installation.
  • For installer or subcontractor applications: trade experience, preferred work locations, licence types and details, availability, ABN status, insurance status, equipment and vehicle information, and any information you choose to provide in the application.
  • Basic website and advertising interaction information, such as referral information or usage data collected through cookies or similar technologies where used.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless we specifically need it and explain why.

3. How we collect information

We collect information when you contact us, submit a website enquiry, request a quote, make a booking, communicate with us, or complete a recruitment application. This includes information submitted through Meta (Facebook or Instagram) Instant Forms. Information may also reach us through service providers that help operate our website, advertising, bookings or communications.

4. Why we use your information

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, provide quotes and services, arrange installations, manage bookings and payments, communicate about jobs, handle customer support, assess installer or subcontractor applications, verify suitable qualifications and insurance where needed, and maintain our business records. We may also use relevant information to improve our services and advertising, subject to applicable law and your choices.

If you submit an installer application, we use your details to evaluate whether you may be suitable for installation work and to contact you about the opportunity. Submitting an application does not guarantee work.

5. Who we share information with

We may disclose relevant details to authorised staff, suitable installers or subcontractors when needed to deliver a service, and to providers that support website hosting, customer enquiries, advertising, communication, scheduling, recordkeeping or payment processing. We may also disclose information where required or permitted by law. We aim to limit shared information to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

When you apply using a Meta Instant Form, Meta also processes information under its own privacy terms. You can read Meta’s Privacy Policy.

6. Storage, security and overseas providers

We take reasonable steps to protect information against misuse, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. Our service providers may store or process some information outside Australia. No internet transmission or storage system is completely risk-free.

7. How long we keep information

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected, for legitimate business records, or to meet legal obligations. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it, subject to applicable retention requirements.

8. Access, correction and your choices

You can contact us to request access to, or correction of, personal information we hold about you. You can also ask us to stop non-essential marketing communications or enquire about deletion of your information. We will consider requests in line with applicable law and any records we are required to retain.

9. Privacy concerns or complaints

If you have a question or complaint about how your information is handled, please email us with enough detail to investigate. We will review your concern and respond within a reasonable period. Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may also be able to raise concerns with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
