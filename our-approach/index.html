The experience matters

We'd Rather Let Our Work and the Experience Speak for Themselves.

We don't think there's much point telling people how good our customer service is. That's something you should be able to judge from dealing with us.

Answering questions properly. Turning up when we've agreed to. Keeping people informed if plans change. And being available when something needs attention.

We understand that sometimes people just need a bit of advice, and other times they're simply looking into their options.

We don't want anyone making a decision blindly.

We might ask you a few questions, but that doesn't mean we're expecting you to buy anything.

The idea is to work together and figure out the best way forward. Sometimes we'll be able to help, sometimes we won't. Either way, we'll be straightforward with you.

We believe the way we handle that first conversation should be the same way we handle things six months after an installation.

And if something goes wrong afterward, there's a process for putting it right.

That's why we have our SplitsPro Workmanship Guarantee. Before starting, we photograph the areas we'll work on and agree on the unit position, pipe routes and wall penetrations. During installation, progress photos are sent back to SplitsPro to check against the agreed plan. Once the job's finished, we check the work and provide photos of the result.

That gives us a record to refer to if there's a concern later. If you report an issue, we'll review it and arrange an inspection where needed. If our workmanship falls short of the agreed plan or the standards covered by our guarantee, we'll return and put it right at no extra cost.

And you don't pay until after the installation is completed.
