Ducted vs Split System Air Conditioning for Sydney Homes

Start with a floor plan and mark the rooms you use at the same time. A family cooling one living room in the afternoon faces a different decision from a family heating four closed bedrooms overnight.

Which layout suits the way you live?

SystemWhere it fitsWhat to check
Single splitOne indoor head and one outdoor unit serve a bedroom or connected living area.Airflow from a hallway rarely reaches a bedroom once the door closes.
Multi-splitSeveral indoor heads share an outdoor unit. Each room can have its own set temperature and schedule, depending on the controller.Most residential multi-splits require active heads to share heating or cooling mode. Ask how much capacity is available when all heads run.
DuctedA concealed indoor unit, ducts and ceiling or floor outlets distribute air from one system with an outdoor unit.Check indoor-unit access, room for ducts, return airflow and which rooms share a zone. Independent room temperatures depend on the controls specified.

Two floor plans, two different quotes

For a Bankstown unit with one open-plan lounge and a separate bedroom, first decide whether the bedroom needs cooling with its door shut. If it does, compare two separate splits with a two-head multi-split. Check the proposed outdoor locations and the building’s approval process before accepting a quote.

For a single-storey home where the family uses the lounge by day and several bedrooms at night, ask for a ducted layout showing the day and night zones. Have the installer explain where air returns when bedroom doors are closed and which outlets must stay open. A suitable route through the ceiling can make a big difference to the quote.

Compare the same rooms and the same scope

A single split usually has the lower upfront cost for one room. Once several rooms need separate heads, price the complete installation both ways. Include pipework, power, outdoor mounts, zoning controls and access for future cleaning. With splits, each indoor head has a filter to maintain. With ducted, ask to see the return filter and the access point for the indoor unit.
