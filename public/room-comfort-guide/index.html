A split system might help. Let's check.

A split system can heat and cool one room. You may not need to change the air conditioning for your whole house. But sometimes a repair or another option is better. First, let's look at the room and what you need.

Start with what makes the room uncomfortable.

One room is too hot even with ducted air conditioning

Check whether the zone is open, the filter is clean and air is reaching the outlet. If it still struggles, have the airflow and heat load assessed before deciding on a separate split system.

A portable or window unit is taking up space

A wall-mounted split system may free up floor or window space. Suitable indoor and outdoor locations, drainage and electrical supply need to be checked first.

Your heater only solves half the problem

A reverse-cycle air conditioner can provide cooling and heating in the same room. The right capacity depends on the space and how you use it.

You're managing without air conditioning

Consider the rooms you use most, the afternoon sun, insulation and the size of the space. Cooling one room may be sufficient, while multiple rooms may need a broader plan.
