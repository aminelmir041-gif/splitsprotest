Best Air Conditioning Brands for Your Sydney Home

For a bedroom, the best buy might be the model you can sleep beside. In a large living area, airflow at the fan speed you actually use may matter more. Start with the room, then compare exact model numbers at the same capacity.

Where to start your shortlist

BrandWhat to compare on the quote
DaikinCompare Cora and Alira X at the capacity you need. Check the exact filtration features, controls and installed price before paying for a range upgrade. See Daikin options.
Mitsubishi ElectricFor a bedroom, ask for the indoor unit’s dimensions, its low-fan sound level and the sound level at your likely operating speed. Confirm whether app control requires an adaptor.
Mitsubishi Heavy IndustriesThis is a separate manufacturer from Mitsubishi Electric. If you’re comparing Avanti and Avanti Plus, check the exact indoor model, available capacity and which features the upgrade adds. See the manufacturer’s split ranges.
RinnaiUse a correctly sized Rinnai as a price comparison. Check the supplied controller, filter maintenance and warranty terms for the quoted series. See Rinnai options.

Use the same test for every brand

  1. Room fit. Check capacity against the room, sun exposure and insulation. Two nominally similar models can have different heating outputs.
  2. Running cost. Compare annual kWh in the same climate zone and the same mode. Read our reverse cycle guide for a worked example.
  3. Sound and controls. Compare indoor sound pressure figures at equivalent fan speeds. Ask whether the quoted Wi-Fi, timer or filtration feature is included on that model.
  4. Installed scope. Include electrical work, pipe length, mounts and old-unit removal where relevant. Ask who handles a product fault and who handles a workmanship issue.

Bring two comparable quotes

Ask each installer to list the indoor and outdoor model numbers, the installed total and the planned outdoor location. If one quote leaves the pipe route or electrical work open, settle those items before choosing the cheaper model.
