01 / Before you commit

The Measure Twice, Cut Once Method.

Our guarantee starts before you've even committed to an installation.

We take the time to understand your home, your comfort needs and what you expect from your air conditioner. We discuss sizing, positioning, noise, appearance, allergies, your family's needs and how you'll use the system.

Not every improvement costs extra. Sometimes it's simply choosing the right features, understanding the trade-offs or positioning the unit differently.

Think about it. If someone takes on your job without asking questions or understanding your needs, that's not expertise — that's poor planning.

We don't want you calling six months later saying, “It's too noisy,” “It doesn't cool properly,” or “I wish we'd installed it somewhere else.” We'd rather prevent problems than come back to fix them.

03 / Your written protection

Our Lifetime Installation Guarantee — In Writing.

Your guarantee is with SplitsPro, not just the installer.

Even if the original installer no longer works with us, SplitsPro remains responsible for honouring our covered workmanship guarantee.

Before work begins, we explain the written guarantee and what it covers. After installation, you receive your SplitsPro Installation Certificate, recording the work completed and checks carried out.

And you don't pay until the installation is finished.

The installation workmanship guarantee and the air conditioner's manufacturer warranty are separate. The written guarantee terms explain the applicable coverage and claim process.

04 / If something goes wrong

What Happens Six Months or Six Years Later?

This is something we explain before the job, not after a problem comes up. You deal directly with SplitsPro — not a subcontractor you have to chase down.

01 / CALL

Tell us what happened.

We ask a few questions and review your installation records.

02 / CHECK

Find the cause.

We arrange an inspection if needed and establish whether it's workmanship, manufacturer warranty or something else.

03 / FIX

If it's our work.

For faults covered by our guarantee, we aim to attend within a week and put our workmanship right at no extra cost.

04 / WARRANTY

If it's the unit.

We help submit the manufacturer claim, aiming to do so within a week once we have the required details.

You shouldn't have to become the project manager of a problem you paid us to solve.

If an issue isn't covered by our installation guarantee, we'll explain our findings and any proposed costs before additional work. Attendance and repair timing can depend on the fault, access and parts.

Why we do it this way

Because good business brings more business. We'd rather invest in looking after our customers than cut costs today and risk letting them down tomorrow.
