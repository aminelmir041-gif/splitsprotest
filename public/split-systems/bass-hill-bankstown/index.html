What will it cost at your place?

A unit on an outside wall with the outdoor unit just behind it is a different job from running the pipes around the house. On a qualifying standard installation, our price includes up to three metres of pipework, up to 20 metres of standard electrical cabling where required, normal mounting, testing and handover.

If access is tight, you need a longer run or the switchboard needs additional work, we explain the cost before you agree. For a replacement, we check the existing supply, isolator, access and position before quoting.

Which size suits the room?

A shaded bedroom and a room with glass doors in full afternoon sun can need different units, even with the same floor area. When you call, tell us the room size, which way the windows face and whether the room opens into the rest of the house. A photo of the wall and the proposed outdoor spot helps us talk through the installation.

What happens before we leave?

We check the finish against the position and pipe route you approved, test the system, show you the controls and give you a written installation certificate. Our lifetime installation guarantee covers SplitsPro’s workmanship. The unit has its own manufacturer’s warranty.
