How much does ducted air conditioning cost?

For a standard installation, our indicative Daikin package with four zones and up to eight outlets is $9,590 for 10 kW, $10,490 for 12.5 kW or $11,290 for 14 kW. Those examples keep the zoning package fixed so you can see the cost of changing capacity.

An outlet is the vent that delivers air. A zone is a room or group of rooms you control together. A large living room can have two outlets on one zone. Your floor plan and the rooms you want to use together help determine both numbers.

What changes the price at your place?

Building a new home

Before the ceilings go in, mark the indoor unit, return-air grille and outlet positions on your floor plan. Ask whether the quote covers both the initial ductwork and the return visit for grilles, controls and commissioning.

Installing in an existing home

A room on the far side of a low roof can need a different duct route. The installer needs to check access through the manhole, available clearance and where the return air can travel before pricing the job.

Replacing an older system

Ask which ducts, dampers and controls can stay. A replacement quote that reuses suitable ducts covers different work from one that removes damaged ductwork and installs new runs.

Compare two written quotes

  • Match the indoor and outdoor model numbers and the cooling capacity in kW.
  • Count the zones and outlets separately. Mark which rooms share a zone.
  • Check the controller, return-air grille and ductwork included in the quoted layout.
  • Ask who pays for electrical upgrades, access work and removal of the old system.
  • Check the total including GST, commissioning, manufacturer warranty and workmanship cover.
