The SplitsPro signature method

We strip it back.
Then clean what you can't see.

A basic filter rinse leaves the buildup inside. Our Bare-Bones Deep Clean goes further, reaching the coil, blower, drain and casing where dust, residue and musty smells can collect.

01 / OPEN IT UP

Strip back the indoor unit

We carefully remove accessible covers, filters, blower components and drain tray where the unit design permits, exposing hidden buildup rather than wiping around it.

02 / DEEP WASH

Enzymes + coil cleaners

We wash the removed parts, indoor coil and air passages with specialist enzyme and coil-cleaning products suited to the surfaces, helping lift dust, grime and residue.

03 / STEAM + DETAIL

Get into the hidden areas

We use controlled steam on suitable components, clean the interior of the case and detail the accessible surfaces so the clean reaches beyond the filters.

04 / DRAIN + OUTSIDE

Clean the full pathway

We clean the drain tray and flush the drain line to clear built-up debris, then tidy the indoor housing and clean the outdoor unit's accessible exterior surfaces.

05 / PROTECT + FINISH

Anti-mould coating and reassembly

We apply a suitable anti-mould coating to appropriate cleaned surfaces, reassemble the system and check operation. We protect your home and leave the work area tidy.
