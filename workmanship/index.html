02 / NEAT WORK & CARE

That wall was fine before.

You get cooling in the living room where your family spends time. But when you get home, the pipe covers are crooked. A big plastic cover runs across your new paint. There are cuts in the wall no one told you about. Now you're worried you'll need another tradie to fix it.

You care about how your home looks. You shouldn't feel bad about a job you paid for each time you walk into the room.

HOW SPLITSPRO HANDLES IT

Before the job, we visit your home. We take photos, measure the walls and mark where the unit will go. We show you where the pipes and plastic pipe covers will run, where the water will drain and where we may need to drill. You get to see the plan before we make a hole.

The installer gets the plan, photos and clear steps for your job. We train our team to measure twice and cut once. That means less mess and less time spent watching over the work.

We send you before-and-after photos of the areas we worked on, even the roof space if we worked there. So you can see what changed. SplitsPro and its installers have cover for property damage, under the terms of their insurance.

You should be able to enjoy the room, not spend your weekend chasing repairs. If our work falls short of the agreed plan, we'll put it right at no extra cost.

03 / CLEAR PRICES

The quote shouldn't grow legs.

You want a split system for the hot bedroom. You make time for a quote, agree on a price and plan the install day. Then the work starts. Now you're told the wall bracket, wires or pipes cost extra. The room is already a mess. You feel stuck.

You don't always want the cheapest job. You want good work for a fair price. What you don't want is a bigger bill that you have to explain at home or argue about later.

HOW SPLITSPRO HANDLES IT

Your time matters. So does ours. On the call and visit, we check the room, where the unit can go, where the pipes will run and what power it needs. Then we give you one clear quote for the work your home needs.

For a standard job, the quote includes the unit, our work, a normal wall bracket or safe spot on the ground, a power shut-off switch and the set amount of pipes and wiring. We tell you how much pipe and wire is included.

If the pipes need to run farther, access is hard or your home needs extra work, we explain it and put it in the quote before you say yes. If we find a new problem later, we tell you the cost first. We won't do extra paid work without your okay.

You know what you're paying for. No time wasted checking a bill you never expected.
